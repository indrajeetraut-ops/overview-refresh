## Assumptions

- **common_residual_window**: common residual window
- **excess_negative_correction**: A notable fraction of its simulated values were negative and corrected to zero.
- **fixed_mix_estimate**: Its token-type mix (input / cache-write 5m / cache-write 1h / cache-read / output) is held fixed at the estimated blend for the whole horizon.
- **scenario_constant_tokens**: Tokens moved to it by a scenario change keep the destination series' own mix (token_factor = 1.0).
- **scenario_propagated_by_share**: Its change at this scope is propagated from a child scope by attribution share, not recomputed independently; exact in expectation, approximate per sample.
- **uncalibrated_model_estimate**: Its forecast probabilities are uncalibrated against held-out data.

- Fan edges shown in charts are per-date quantiles across samples, not one coherent future path.
- Organization, Team, and Project scopes overlap; their totals must not be added together.
- Prices are the latest known at the time this run was priced; a later price change is not reflected here.
- Long-context pricing tiers are ignored.
- The agent x token-type split used here may differ from the forecast report's own split (forecasting's category allocation uses a different, fixed 28-day plain-sum estimator) -- a known error.
